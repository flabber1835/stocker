# P01 grouped factual synthetic components

The grouped-input qualifier calls `qualify_components(binding, template_state,
output)` only inside its fenced hosted worker after the exact existing 516/224
epoch and historical quote contexts are installed. The template is a canonically
restored complete SessionState from the input zero. It supplies identity and
spectator controller/history fields, not future holdings. Every component book,
order, price, ledger setup and date is explicitly synthetic. These fixtures are
disposable, encrypted evidence and never a financial predecessor or input.
The configuration is the canonical funding factory selected by the exact
admitted parent's policy, including its 30 slots and research economic profile;
the generic V5 default is not the component configuration. Its complete fields,
policy and configuration hash are recorded. Reports use the actual canonical
Mark type with explicit synthetic current raw prices.

The component imports canonical modules only after the hosted fence. It binds
their actual bytes to the current source map. An AST extraction takes the exact
canonical `adapter.step_session` prefix through `_resolved_open_equity`, stopping
before pending fills, aging or decisions. It preserves the existing metadata,
split, dividend, ordinary terminal and pending-transform callsites. This is a
source-bound action-phase qualifier, not another session engine. No canonical
module or global hook is modified.

Three valid complete SessionState fixtures contain, respectively, an existing
delivered-security holding, its queued CLOSE, or a reserved dollar BUY. The
source is unheld and every source intent absent. Running the canonical action
prefix with and without the ordinary source term must preserve the entire
SessionState and ledger, including target reservations and every pending field.
Both results must roundtrip through canonical full-state/ledger decoding. A
disjoint mutated copy of the exact pending-transform AST compares intent identity
to the target instead of the source; its full-state result must differ. The
qualified function and canonical module globals remain unchanged afterward.

Four ordinary held conversion cases use the exact legal ratios and synthetic
quantities/prices, and each also contains a preexisting seven-share target
holding. That separate target episode must remain byte-identical while the
source episodes convert; the report and aggregate ledger count both the old
target quantity and the newly delivered quantity. This qualifies only the
held-source/existing-target-holding composition. A target pending order in a
held-source conversion remains unqualified and refuses separately.
HOLDER aggregation determines the integral delivered total
once. The canonical allocator then distributes that total across episodes in
sorted slot order and assigns the final floating residual to the last episode.
An aggregate whole number does not prove exact episode representation. A case
qualifies only when every actual allocation equals its rational entitlement as
an exact binary64 value, and their sum exactly conserves the holder total.

The initial-share reference has a separate canonical rule:
`max(1, int(round(initial_shares * float(ratio))))`. Each rational initial
reference must be a positive integer and the canonical expression must equal it.
For example, VSLR two current lots of 10 deliver 5.5 each, an exact binary64
representation of 11 aggregate shares. Initial lots of 10 would round to 6 each
and are refused; synthetic initial lots of 20 with a prior partial sale to 10
preserve initial references of 11 each. BMCH and WPX also exercise exact half-share
episode allocations. WLH exercises its distinct legal cash plus stock package.

Canonical conversion, cash allocation, episode references, age/review fields,
source-lot provenance, ledger, complete SessionState roundtrip, opening accounting
and final report must agree with independent checks. Quantity and initial-count
checks use exact rational/binary64 equality. Price references retain the exact
existing canonical operation order: pure stock uses the reciprocal of the
binary64 ratio, while mixed consideration uses its existing cash-adjusted rebase.
For WPX the former differs from rounding an ideal rational reciprocal; this
component preserves and records that canonical operation rather than introducing
a new arithmetic model, tolerance or claim of exact real-valued price arithmetic.
The common CIL
price remains absent and aggregate fractional entitlements refuse. Source pending
orders, source episode exit flags, inexact episode allocations and unsupported
initial-reference rounding are negative cases, with the complete prior state and
ledger unchanged. An actual removal of the allocation guard must accept the bad
synthetic representation, proving the falsifier reaches that guard. No epsilon,
rounded financial quantity, new cash-in-lieu rate or inferred future own role is
admitted.

The component writes `target-independence-component.json.gz`, with durable
per-case receipts before the summary. It returns
`target_independence_qualified` and `ordinary_representation_qualified` only after
all canonical cases and falsifiers pass. The actual February 5/6 session path,
per-use guard, fresh finance equality and stopped canonical review remain
separate runtime gates.
