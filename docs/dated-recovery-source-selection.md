# Dated recovery inputs must match the selected strategy

Decision: 2026-10-13. After a next market open passes, retained GO uses historical reconstruction rather than prospective execution. On installed `abeac17c`, this path selected Monday's authenticated legacy publication because it was the single dated publication after Friday's book. Its acquisition strategy digest belonged to an earlier executable and differed from both the selected strategy and the exact retained checkpoint. The unchanged strict readiness reader correctly refused `ROLLING_ACQUISITION_STRATEGY_CHANGED`. The original book, account binding and failed cycles remained intact.

For a new reconstruction step, select dated publications whose acquisition request binds the actual selected production strategy. Filter the dated publication inventory through its operational snapshot and preparation job before applying the existing uniqueness rule. Reauthenticate the selected publication, its dated receipt, candidate, validation and request binding exactly as before. A missing compatible dated publication remains `MISSING_DATED_PUBLICATION`; the existing coordinator may then acquire an explicitly marked current-information recovery generation under the selected strategy and original bounded request deadline. It must never relabel the older publication, its strategy or availability timestamp. Two eligible dated publications remain an ambiguity refusal, and malformed or tampered selected bindings remain hard refusals.

Trailing candidates and already recorded historical checkpoints continue through their exact authenticated checkpoint/publication and runtime admission path. They are not new publication selection. Their state, original runtime, economic identity, HMAC records and replay remain unchanged. The strict acquisition-strategy guard remains in the readiness reader; filtering cannot substitute for that guard or grant historical performance, prospective authority or a broker operation.

Extend the existing exact close-cycle upgrade profile only with the reviewed reconstruction-selector module. Pin the authenticated original and preceding executable source hashes and exact repaired destination bytes. Do not widen the administrative source allowlist, change economic migration profiles or reinterpret an unknown executable as compatible. Documentation and the profile must precede a delivered executable admission.

Qualification covers selection with a stale sole publication, compatible and stale publications together, duplicate compatible publications, tampered request/receipt refusal, exact checkpoint crash/restart, current-information acquisition after an expired open, preserved old records and zero broker commands. A falsifier removing strategy filtering must fail the stale-publication case. Use isolated small fixtures and the actual production hashes read-only; do not create another full production diagnostic copy. Protected certification, installed full financial GO, exact semantic restore and ordinary supported activation remain required.
