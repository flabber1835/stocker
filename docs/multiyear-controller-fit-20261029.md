# Multi-year controller study: revision and data feasibility

Status: design and source feasibility only. No corrected numerical protocol or
new financial origin is selected yet. This supersedes the one-year period
proposal in the earlier source-correction chapter. All original protocols and
failed artifacts remain immutable.

The owner questioned fitting 108 risk settings on one year while longer history
exists. The one-year choice was a runtime compromise after the request for a
short initial pass; it is insufficient for durable risk-rule selection. Preserve
the same 108 existing Owned55 settings and improve calculation reuse rather than
using one year's behavior as evidence of general robustness.

The candidate period design is five full training years, 2018–2022, evaluation
over 2023–2024 and a later known-history diagnostic over 2025–October 2026.
Report annual subperiods separately. Initialization uses earlier 299-feature and
126-formation observations. Exact dates and execution bounds must be frozen in
a new protocol before financial dispatch. This is a proposed replacement, not a
claim that the corrected study has run or that these periods are unseen.

Keep the owner's existing-provider constraint: Alpaca market data and anonymous
free OpenFIGI classification. Official Alpaca documentation lists stock history
since 2016. Verify actual action and temporal-identity coverage separately;
bar availability alone does not establish either. The already-held 2008–2026
archive has a different frozen provider/model basis and must not silently become
an Alpaca control or be joined into a mixed-provider economic path.

The original Alpaca trace is factually invalid: unrelated current CLBR and PEW
identities share earlier observations, including hundreds before current CLBR's
issuer existed. Retained controller-state influence is documented privately. A conditional
accounting/restore pass is not proof of true issuer identity. Preserve that trial
as failed evidence; do not advance or score its state. A corrected source trial
needs a distinct dataset identity and fresh canonical formation.

Before choosing its new origin, diagnose temporal identities from frozen current
FIGIs, exact corporate-action preimages and primary issuer boundaries. Current
old-symbol listings cannot borrow departed issuers' history. Changed-CUSIP
successors need conservative supported history boundaries. A later separate
common-share record may provide a conservative admission boundary, not a made-up
company inception date. Unknown reused-issuer boundaries quarantine the current
ID. A same-day old-unit record cannot establish a later issuer. Duplicate current
share classes must not supply multiple universe votes. Missing or malformed
terms remain unknown. Prices, holdings and candidate performance must not choose
these metadata rules. Source collision evidence may flag ambiguity, but does not
authorize merging companies or fabricating prices.

The source-only diagnostic module does not integrate a new policy into Core or
start any financial calculation. Its proposal still requires complete raw and
metadata provenance, final policy review and a new dataset commitment. Revised
history and current-cohort survivorship remain explicit limitations.

Useful feasibility work may make a small bounded read-only request to Alpaca's
market-data bars/actions endpoints, using the existing provider-key loader. Do
not call account, order, position, activity or database APIs. Preserve exact
response hashes and keep payloads private/encrypted. No credentials may enter
public logs or financial workers. Local source parsing is permitted; financial
Core/controller/account replay and containers remain isolated GitHub compute.

After the four-request coverage sample, a metadata-only extension may retrieve
structural action types from 2015 through the fixed October 27, 2026 cutoff.
Limit this to 240 pages and 600 seconds, preserve partial responses and explicit
pagination status, and make no price or account calculation. The purpose is to
identify older ticker departures/current-issue boundaries before deciding the
new dataset. It does not qualify the complete action history or authorize a
financial run. Keep its exact raw responses and hashes in the private case.

PAPER, main, broker state, databases and shared Docker/WSL/services remain
untouched. Only the research branch and PR 542 carry changes. No production
certification, deployment, promotion or automatic parameter-family expansion is
authorized by this revision.
