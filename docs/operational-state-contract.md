Retain one spawned operational collection child within the existing verifier

## Diagnosable finite verification

financial observer's child boundary capture a closed, bounded Pydantic diagnostic
envelope before generic process error wrapping. Record controlled outcome/code,
phase, original exception type and a SHA256 diagnostic fingerprint; never persist
raw driver text, DSN, credentials, account payload or traceback. Parent-owned
attempt identity, subject, admission/completion time and original deadline remain
distinct from the child failure. Successful observer/result and persisted
financial receipt formats retain compatibility.
For a completed negative result without an exception, fingerprint the bounded
negative-row key/status/value/detail and source-error JSON before discarding it.
Only the digest is diagnostic metadata; neither the original text nor account
values enter the attempt history. This distinguishes different negative causes
without treating a successful metric or its refreshed timestamp as the cause.

Distinguish a completed negative financial result, read unavailability, transport
timeout/failure and superseded work. A subject changed before/during observation
earns no current proof and is classified SUPERSEDED. Rechecking retains the
original admitted queue-plus-work maximum (600 seconds), anchored at the first
superseded check, with the existing 30-second retry cadence and one execution
slot. Repeated subject churn cannot renew checking indefinitely. An expired
anchor, clock regression, malformed outcome or known prior failure remains a
failure until a complete current verification succeeds. No stale green or
negative-result suppression is introduced.

Retain structured diagnostics independently of the replaceable financial result.
Use only `DIAGNOSTIC:SHADOW` and `DIAGNOSTIC:PAPER` rows in the existing receipt
table, with schema `sentinel.financial-observer-diagnostics/1` and HMAC purpose

Keep the latest strictly validated parent-owned attempt and at most sixteen
unique failed-attempt summaries. Each summary preserves its first controlled

later success, supersession, polling and process restart cannot erase that first

## Qualification and delivery

### Typed row facts and producer ownership

Before implementation, select one closed `OperationalFact` schema on `Row`.
Its kind is one of AUTOMATION_POLICY, AUTOMATION_CYCLE, CONTROLLER_DECISION,
MARKET_DATA, SHADOW_VERIFICATION or PAPER_VERIFICATION. Kind-specific closed
state sets and a coherence validator reject foreign combinations or unrelated
fields. Dated source facts carry an ISO decision/session date; market data also
carries the measured nonnegative session lag, and controller decisions carry
their bounded Decimal exposure and explicit adoption state. Automation policy
records enabled/released, inert or killed; cycle facts record native durable
states or the explicit discovery wait. PAPER verification distinguishes pending
from checking. These are display inputs, never execution grants.

The existing native producers own these facts: automation policy/cycle/step
constructors, controller exposure, accepted feed and shadow verification
constructors, the pending PAPER constructor and the independent checking
adapter. Allowed row keys are checked on construction and decoding. Formatters
cannot manufacture them from `value`, `detail` or labels. The common classifier
uses those facts with all existing clocks, recovery, account, plan and negative
evidence guards. Missing facts in historical receipts remain readable, but
cannot earn a normal-work exception by matching old display copy. Nested fact
decoding is explicit in the legacy observer codec and typed in the dashboard
envelope. This replaces string-dependent health decisions without adding a
normal-work allowance.
