# Reuse one published snapshot measurement in the SHADOW verifier

The November 20 PAPER opening spent 23.9 seconds constructing its broker adapter.
The resolver needs only published reference and alias evidence, but its shared
input helper loaded and verified about 1.6 million price rows. Construct symbol
resolvers against the authenticated current publication and its nonretired
candidate. Authenticate the manifest and reference, validation, source and
alias evidence already used for resolver construction. Keep the full price
check in marks, action and cash consumers and execution readiness. Neither the
resolver nor its references grant transport authority.

Periodic SHADOW status re-observation currently discards the raw readiness
material at every bounded subprocess exit. Preserve that material in one
fixed-size, authenticated shared-memory slot owned by this existing verifier
incarnation, following the PAPER mirror classifier's ownership pattern. A slot
is only passed to SHADOW observers. Execution, standalone callers, formation,
reconstruction, restore, full audits and the canonical publisher keep their
current verification paths. This memo carries the underlying measured inputs
for the existing readiness clauses. Those clauses, current source frontier,
publication receipt chain, shadow record, account boundary and observer owner
checks still run on their original clocks.

Only the same exact published ALPACA_OPENFIGI normalized generation qualifies.
Read the witness from the verifier's repeatable-read, read-only proof while its
original corpus pin is held. Require the independent public DDL witness and the
normalized layout/catalog; bind cluster, database, postmaster startup, XID
wrap epoch, relation/TOAST identities, exact publication and all its candidate,
comparison and normalized block evidence row versions. Detect payload removal,
substitution and rewrite through those identities, and refuse legacy payload
mixed into the candidate. Normal supported writes cannot modify a sealed live
block. A catalog or row version change gets a fresh full scan. Privileged bypass
of all catalog and operational guards is outside the installation contract.

Publish a completed measurement only after recapturing the same witness under
the existing pin. Cache neither exceptions nor partial results. A hit does not
advance the measurement's clocks. Force byte revalidation after twenty minutes
from the scan's original start on either wall or monotonic time, leaving room
for the ordinary broker-free verifier cadence. This bounds detection of changes
that do not change a PostgreSQL logical row witness. A new verifier incarnation
always starts cold. A malformed, missing, oversized or unavailable memo falls
back to canonical verification. Receipt/durable storage formats do not change.

Qualification will count complete scans across independent verifier attempts,
exercise expiry and failed renewal, and detect same-count block substitution and
physical rewrites. It will assert the symbol-reader path has no price scan and
that the normal execution readiness and marked-price checks still verify rows.
