## Canonical C operation

Introduce research-only `REDUCE_POSITION`/`CONCENTRATION_BAND` operations. Only
the C profile calls the trim planner after ordinary exit/review decisions and
before admissions. Aggregate all episodes per security; strictly above 10% of
resolved Core NAV, close-bind the floor(8% NAV/raw close) remaining quantity.
Existing full exits and queued reductions take precedence; do not issue duplicate
writers/intents or spend expected sale cash before its actual next-open fill.

Allocate the residual exactly pro rata using rational arithmetic. The canonical
engine reduction transition preserves episode entry, age, review, peak and lot
provenance, sells the positive difference at raw open, charges the existing fees
and posts SELL ledger evidence. A positive residual starts no cooldown. A zero
target uses ordinary full-exit and both cooldowns. The target is not recomputed
at a gapped open. Splits transform queued quantities through the unchanged
canonical split path before execution. Pending full closes supersede trims.

The existing canonical quantity store uses decimal-spelled floats. The initial
screen refuses a pro-rata residual or sale that cannot be represented exactly in
that store; it never rounds away an entitlement or adjusts the last lot to hide
the discrepancy. This is an explicit representation limit, not an economic loss
or permission to skip the trim. A pending trim intersecting a terminal identity
conversion also refuses until its exact conversion semantics are separately
qualified. Cash terminal removal can extinguish the obsolete trim through the
ordinary terminal cancellation path. Any such refusal is engineering evidence;
the affected arm has no admitted economic result. Do not silently broaden these
limits after observing performance.

inactive hooks do not alter their financial state. V restoration validates paired
