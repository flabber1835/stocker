# P00 volatility-penalty replication

## Owner authorization and fixed question, 2026-11-08

The owner authorized this single experiment after a read-only review of P00 and
the eleven other saved Core variants. Test whether reducing the volatility
penalty in entry ranking improves compound growth, both in Core alone and with
the existing unchanged Sentinel. Do not change Sentinel to fit the alternative.
The earlier twelve-variant campaign remains paused and its files, artifacts,
checkpoints and unsent tails remain intact. This is a distinct research lineage.

The observational lead is that P00's entries with lower recorded formation
volatility had weak subsequent price returns and occupied substantial capital.
That does not establish an improvement from changing the score: the same cohort
contains important long-term winners. Earlier action-normalized research also
reported a worse result for a volatility exponent of 0.75. This experiment tests
that causal gap with the canonical research implementation; it is not a sweep,
a new threshold search, a production change or a promise of improvement.
