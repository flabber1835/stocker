## Questions and method

The owner's clarified objective is production mechanical fitness, not explaining
or recovering the reference multiple. Attribution only selects diagnostic windows.
An optimal portfolio policy is not identifiable from these observations. Assess
the narrower, testable questions: timely response to sustained owned losses,
false exits, recovery delays, capital deployment, and sensitivity to book age.
