## Recovery evidence

warning, keyed by that warning's immutable alert ID. Require a subsequent
canonical `SHADOW_GO` / `VERIFIED` result for the matching actual current closed

process, resumed download, successful HTTP request, waiting result, unrelated

strategy update; it does not claim that the separate next-open plan or orders

Legacy generic dependency warnings remain immutable. They may earn a recovery

incident. A later ordinary verified invocation may retry recovery publication.

leadership, integrity, broker-UNKNOWN and critical-alert policy are unchanged.
