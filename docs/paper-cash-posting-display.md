# Unconfirmed cash activity in the PAPER dashboard

An executed dividend in Alpaca's activity stream can be absent from account
cash and the dividend history. The November 17 account reported $7,064.26
in both cash and buying power. The stream reported a $6.25 credit and a
$0.01 debit. Treat the credit as unconfirmed and reserve the possible debit.

The account card shows the broker's reported cash and buying power. For an
admitted plan with outstanding cash events it also shows the unconfirmed
credits, reserved debits and original execution reconciliation time. Keep these
amounts separate, including when they offset. The displayed upper bound uses
the lower of the reported buying power and cash after reserved debits. Other
order reservations and headroom can reduce what an individual order may spend.

Render this information from the current plan and bound account's durable
execution evidence. A later valuation snapshot cannot refresh the posting
timestamp. If an expected evidence read fails, report the failure. A posting
with known economics has no independent severity while the execution policy
admits normal operation. Existing authority, ownership, reconciliation,
freshness and operator failures continue to determine health. A still-fenced
install cannot turn green through this display.

During the opening window, an otherwise clean purchase attempt may have
insufficient confirmed funds to submit even the minimum order. Show the
executor's qualified cash wait and its original cutoff. An expired window,
unknown order, unrelated funding shortage or contradictory receipt retains its
own status. A wait records that the purchase is still outstanding.
