# Activation over a retained rolling book

[Late cash distributions](late-cash-distributions.md) adds a separately pinned
economic-policy migration. It is not administrative compatibility: new forward
states carry the target identity while the original book and genesis remain.

Software installation remains independent of market time, market data and paper
authority. A separately authorized activation may encounter a book established
by an earlier activation attempt. It must authenticate and continue that book;
it must not require an empty strategy database, repeat formation, reset a binding
or manufacture another genesis. Unknown, corrupt or foreign lineage still
refuses. A returning book is not evidence of paper execution readiness.

## Economic identity and executable admission

The executable identity continues to cover the complete current Sentinel and
Wealth Core sources, exact immutable image, revision, dependency closure, image
lock, interpreter and calendar. It is never replaced with an older image's
identity. A book's immutable origin, decisions, inputs, runtime attestations and
economic strategy identity retain the identities under which they were made.

Three administrative seams are separated from economic source identity: the
source-identity recipe, checkpoint restoration's runtime-admission hook, and the
advancing-checkpoint loader's same hook. Their economic source contribution is
the reviewed canonical baseline only when the entire remaining AST is identical
to that baseline and the added hook has the exact documented syntax. Otherwise
their actual source hash contributes, changing strategy identity and refusing
reuse. All other economic modules and Wealth Core keep their exact source-byte
identities. This recipe is deterministic and never reads a broker or database.
The full executable hash always records the actual administrative code.
The replaced source-identity recipe also requires its exact reviewed function
header: no arguments, defaults, decorators, type parameters or changed return
annotation may be hidden by replacing its body. These can change executable
behavior and therefore cannot inherit the old economic contribution.

Admitting a different executable is an explicit, broker-free operation while
automation is disabled and killed, under the existing behavioral writer lock and
backup authority. An untrusted Git source manifest is accepted only if its full
path/hash closure reproduces the HMAC-authenticated origin's Sentinel hash.
Compare it with the actual current image: all changes must be in the reviewed
administrative seams, supervision, or the new admission/proof modules. Removed
files, economic changes, foreign additions and unexplained changes refuse.
Substitute only that proven original source hash/count into the actual current
environment record; its digest must reproduce the authenticated origin's
environment digest. Dependency, calendar, interpreter or base-image changes
therefore refuse. Capital, observation, model, timing policy and complete
economic strategy identity must remain equal.

Authenticate the complete original checkpoint, bounded current record/input
closure and runtime-attestation chain before adding an append-only HMAC admission
row. Bind it to the immutable origin and the actual new executable and reviewed
configuration. Its namespace is administrative, grants no SHADOW_GO or broker
authority and does not alter financial rows. Retries recover the exact admission;
readers require its exact current-process binding before restoring historical
book provenance. A changed publication subject is not a new genesis.

## GO and paper candidate evidence

GO dispatches fresh state to the existing formation path and authenticated
retained state to canonical rolling continuation/reconstruction. Same-session
work verifies without acquisition or another decision. A gap retains the ordinary
dated-input and no-backdating rules. Readiness, source-final availability,
following-open limits, exclusive ownership and backup renewal remain enforced.

Retained parity is a distinct scope. Read the authenticated current closure and
its immediate predecessor (or the authenticated formed seed for the origin).
Decode the archived exact input; execute the canonical transition on the native
and serialized predecessor, and require both to equal the immutable current
state/decision. This is one read-only bounded transition and restart proof, not
another 126-session formation or historical performance certification. Missing
predecessor/input evidence refuses. GO and the paper-candidate validator accept
only the matching retained proof schema and bindings; a retained report cannot
stand in for fresh formation. Candidate creation independently rechecks the
current retained evidence before issuance.

Activation still requires its own exact GO bundle, current readiness, account
reconciliation, signed paper authority and full semantic restore of the admitted
runtime/state before kill release. An expired historical decision never becomes
a prospective order. No installation receipt is rewritten by these operations.
