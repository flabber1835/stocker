# Champion path-divergence plateau targeted experiment

Purpose: test whether Caesar 20 sits inside a stable local behavioral basin or at a path bifurcation boundary.

Preregistered portfolio sizes: 19, 21, 23.

Only permitted economic changes per arm:
- `N_SLOTS = size`
- `ENTRY_W = 1 / size`

Everything else remains pinned to the corrected certified 25-stock baseline, including exact one-session dividend settlement, factual security classification, 21-session cooldown, 119-session review, 30% trailing stop, one-at-a-time initialized admission throttle, immutable canonical PIT corpus, and full 2006-07-31 through 2026-07-31 replay.

Primary post-run analysis is structural: holdings Jaccard similarity, smaller-book overlap, allocation-state agreement, entry/exit agreement, daily-return correlation, tracking error, NAV-ratio dispersion, and persistence of path divergence versus neighboring completed sizes.

Decision question: determine whether 19-21 forms a stable local basin around Caesar 20 and whether 22-25 forms a continuous stable basin through size 23.

No production-promotion decision is encoded in this experiment.
